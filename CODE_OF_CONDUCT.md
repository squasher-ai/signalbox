# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility and apologizing to people affected by our mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, sexualized language or imagery, public or private abuse, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through the repository's private GitHub security advisory channel. Project maintainers will review reports, protect the reporter's privacy, and explain any enforcement decision when it is safe to do so.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
